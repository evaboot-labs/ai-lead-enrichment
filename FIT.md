# Who fits

Status: EXAMPLE

The agent reads this file before it judges an enriched contact. Replace the example with your own buyers: run the skill and it asks you, or edit the file yourself and set `Status: yours`.

## Buyers

- Heads of Sales, Sales Ops, RevOps, SDR managers and founders.
- At B2B software companies of 51 to 500 employees, with a sales team of 5 or more.

## Not a fit

- Engineering, design, support and finance roles.
- Rows that are not a person: a team, a shared inbox or a company page.

## Fields

- `job_function`: a short department name (Sales, Marketing, Engineering...), only when a title is known.
- `hs_seniority`: one of HubSpot's values: owner, partner, executive, vp, director, manager, senior, entry, employee.
- `fit`: good, maybe or no. Without a title, the fit is maybe at best.
- `summary`: one line under 30 words, from the facts only. Never guess anyone's gender.
